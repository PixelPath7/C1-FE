Residents

Keepers and denizens of an ancient realm, who embodyeth mighty forces and timeless mysteries, bound in their sacred roles within the shadowed depths of the Netherworld.

HADES: The god of the Underworld and absolute ruler of the souls of the dead. He resides in his majestic palace, overseeing the fate of souls and watching over the regions of the Underworld.

Persephone: Queen of the Underworld and wife of Hades. Her duality between life and death makes her a unique figure, connecting fertility and rebirth with the realm of the dead.

Cerberus: The three-headed dog guarding the gates of the Underworld, ensuring no souls escape and no living beings enter without permission. A symbol of death's inevitability and Hades' loyal protector.

Thanatos: The personification of peaceful and inevitable death. Son of Nyx and brother of Hypnos, he is a calm yet relentless spirit who guides souls to their final destination.

Hypnos: The peaceful god of sleep, son of Nyx and twin brother of Thanatos. He lives in the Underworld near the River Lethe, surrounded by tranquil plants that induce rest.

Nyx: The primordial goddess of night, born from Chaos and mother to Hypnos and Thanatos. She resides in the Underworld, embodying mystery and influencing the darker aspects of existence.
